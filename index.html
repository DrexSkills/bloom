Help & support

Logging a period. Tap Log period on Home on the first day. When it’s over, open it again and tap Period ended.

Fixing a date. Open Calendar, tap the day, and choose what it should be. Or edit it in History.

Predictions. After two logged periods, your average cycle length is used. Until then, the length in Cycle settings is used.

Add to Home Screen. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

This app isn’t birth control and can’t diagnose anything. If something feels off, talk to a doctor.
